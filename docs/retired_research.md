# Retired research branches

## 2026-09-14 — independent manifold / latent-state experiments

Retired at the user's request. The independent experimental package, five CLI
entry points, five test modules, eleven proposal/protocol/findings documents,
and generated experiment directories were removed from the active workspace.
Their type-check configuration was removed. No daily research runner, original
financial representation, accounting Innovation/sigma, or trading permission
was changed.

Reason: synthetic alarm calibration did not clear the complete reliability
criteria; the subsequent real-data development replay did not establish
incremental return-prediction value. On 90 scored events / 46 issuers / two
quarters, combined Ridge increased MAE by 4.66% vs filing-only; two-component
PCA increased it by 3.24%. The primary paired error interval crossed zero.
These were previously observed historical outcomes, NOT new validation, and
must never be recycled as untouched evidence. This does not refute all state
representations or deep learning.

Original SEC, licensed market data, financial forecasts, ordinary pre-filing
momentum/volatility features, and the prospective-event pipeline remain intact.
In particular, `sec_data/validation_market_state.py` predates this experiment
and is still required by the financial/guidance research; it is NOT the retired
latent-state package.

A SHA-256-verified, Git-ignored LOCAL archive preserves all removed source,
protocol, and result files at
`data/processed/retired_research/market-manifold-2026-09-14.zip`.
Its `RETIREMENT_MANIFEST.json` records original relative paths and exact hashes.
The archive is evidence/backup only: it is not imported, tested, scheduled, or
part of an active research roadmap. Do not silently restore or deploy it.
